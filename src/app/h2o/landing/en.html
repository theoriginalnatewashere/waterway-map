UNDERSTANDING TDS METER READINGS

What do TDS ranges mean for RO water?

A TDS meter measures dissolved substances in parts per million (ppm), not a cleanliness percentage. For RO dispensers, H2O Map uses the following ranges as a preliminary guide to changes in filtration performance.

H2O Map TDS screening ranges
0–20 ppmExcellent
21–40 ppmVery Good
41–60 ppmGood
61–100 ppmFilter Inspection Needed
> 100 ppmFilter Replacement Needed

How to interpret a reading

A lower number means fewer dissolved substances, not proof that water is free of pathogens or hazardous chemicals.

A noticeable rise from the same dispenser’s earlier readings warrants checking the RO system and comparing the incoming water. Post-filter remineralization can also increase TDS.

WHO / 2026

WHO guidance addresses drinking-water taste

WHO describes water below approximately 600 mg/L as generally palatable and above approximately 1,000 mg/L as increasingly unpalatable. These values concern taste, not safety; WHO does not propose a health-based TDS limit. WHO Guidelines for Drinking-water Quality (2026), p. 246

Important: TDS is not a drinking-water safety testTDS alone cannot establish drinking-water safety or detect bacteria, viruses, heavy metals or specific contaminants.

The 0–20, 21–40, 41–60, 61–100 and over-100 ppm bands are H2O Map labels, not WHO safety limits or a definitive diagnosis that a filter needs replacing.

Note: For dilute drinking water, meter readings in ppm are approximately equivalent to mg/L.

03 / How

Find. Check. Contribute.

Three straightforward steps to take part in the community water map.

STEP 01

Explore the map

Use your location to find nearby RO dispensers and view their reported status.

STEP 02

Review observations

Open a dispenser to see reported TDS, pH, photos, and the date of the latest report.

STEP 03

Add your report

Submit measurements and observations to help the community keep the map current.

About water quality: TDS and pH measurements are screening indicators only. They do not confirm that drinking water is safe or detect bacteria, viruses, heavy metals, and other specific contaminants.
04 / Install

Keep the map on your home screen.

H2O Map works as a Progressive Web App (PWA). Add it to your phone's home screen for quicker access without searching an app store.

On iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open H2O Map in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

On Android (Chrome)

  1. Open H2O Map in Chrome.
  2. Tap the browser's three-dot menu.
  3. Select Install app or Add to Home screen, then confirm.
